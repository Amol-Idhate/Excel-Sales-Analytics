# Excel Sales & Finance Analytics

## About the Project

This project is based on sales and finance data analysis using Microsoft Excel.

The main goal was to understand how the business was performing across
different customers, markets, and time periods, and to present the findings
in a simple and useful way.

The project covers two main areas:

- Sales Analysis
- Finance Analysis

---

## Sales Analysis

### What I worked on

- Customer-wise sales performance
- Market performance compared with targets
- Year-over-year sales comparison
- Identifying changes in sales performance across customers and markets

### Reports

- [Customer Performance Report](./Customer%20Performance%20Report.pdf)
- [Market Performance vs Target Report](./Market%20Performance%20vs%20Target%20Report.pdf)

These reports helped me look at questions such as:

- Which customers are contributing more to sales?
- How has sales performance changed over the years?
- Which markets are performing above or below their targets?
- Where can the business focus for further improvement?

## What I Learned

While working on this project, I got practical experience in taking business
data, preparing it for analysis, and turning it into reports that are easier
to understand.

Some of the key areas I practiced were:

- Cleaning and transforming data
- Working with large datasets
- Creating a date table using Power Query
- Working with fiscal months and quarters
- Building relationships using Power Pivot
- Creating calculated columns using DAX
- Analyzing sales and financial performance
- Building reports around business requirements

---

## Key Takeaways

This project helped me understand that good reporting is not only about
creating tables or charts. The important part is being able to look at the
data from the right business perspective and find information that can help
with decision-making.
